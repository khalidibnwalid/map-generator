Fantasy World Map Generator

A free, in-browser procedural map generator for worldbuilders, game masters and writers. Generate a continent, watch nations rise and fall, and export a poster.

Features

  • Earthlike tectonic continents, Pangea and island worlds with rivers, lakes, biomes and ocean currents
  • Nations, provinces, cultures, flags, great powers and diplomacy
  • Cities with procedural street plans, roads, sea lanes and trade flows
  • Seasons, night mode with city lights and drifting weather
  • History simulation with wars, battles, dynasties and a chronicle
  • High-resolution poster export, animated GIF timelapse and .world save files

Frequently asked questions

Is the Fantasy World Map Generator free?

Yes. It is free, open source and runs entirely in your browser with no account or upload.

Can I export my fantasy map as a poster?

Yes. The File tab exports a high-resolution poster with a title, legend, compass rose and frame, and nation fact sheets.

Will the same seed always make the same map?

Yes. Generation is deterministic, and a .world save file stores the seed plus every edit you made.

Can it simulate the history of my world?

Yes. The History tab runs year-by-year expansion, wars, battles, collapses, dynasties and rulers. You can replay any past year and export the timeline as an animated GIF.

What do I need to run it?

A modern browser with JavaScript and WebGL2. Nothing is sent to a server.
